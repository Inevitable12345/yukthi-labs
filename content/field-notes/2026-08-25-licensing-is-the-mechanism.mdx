Supply-chain risk in critical minerals is almost always framed geologically: who has the deposits,
what the reserves are, how long they last. That framing produces a slow variable — reserves change
over decades — and therefore a slow risk model.

The April 2025 export licensing regime does not act on that variable at all. <EvidenceMarker id="E-004" />
Nothing about deposits changed. Nothing about refining capacity changed either.
What changed was the time between an application and a granted licence.

## Why the distinction matters for a causal model

A resource constraint and an administrative constraint produce different graphs.

- A resource constraint binds at the mine, moves slowly, and is visible in reserve data years ahead.
- An administrative constraint binds at the border, can move within a week, and is visible only in
  lead times — which most procurement systems record as supplier performance rather than as policy.

The second is the one that actually bound. It is also the one that a model built on the first
representation cannot express, because there is no node in it for a licence.

## What makes it a chokepoint

Concentration is the enabling condition rather than the cause. Roughly 90% of global rare-earth
refining capacity sits in one jurisdiction. <EvidenceMarker id="E-005" /> A licensing requirement
imposed where processing is distributed would be absorbed by substitution; imposed where it is
concentrated, it propagates.

That is a joint condition — concentration _and_ control — which is exactly the shape a hyperedge
exists to represent. Either alone is survivable.

## The observation

The IEA characterised 2025 as the point at which a long-documented concentration risk stopped being
theoretical. <EvidenceMarker id="E-016" /> Nothing in the structure changed on the day of the
announcement. The concentration had been there for years, and so had the dependency.

What changed was the **state of an edge**, from latent to active, with no node changing at all.
A monitoring system that watches nodes and not edges records nothing on that day.
